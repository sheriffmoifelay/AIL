X - MART SUPERMARKET

X - Mart is the first supermarket in Buduburam. It was first located opposite Liberia Camp, adjacent Former Ecobank Building along with which were demolished due to the road construction which started in 2024. It has been a source of employment for many youths in its environment including foreign nationals hence its employment is not limited to only Ghannaian nationals.
X - Mart has been an entry level for so many job seekers and has empowered many people to do business of their own after their stay.

It has been a long time customer to distribution companies in the region like FanMilk, Detella, Naatoa, Kofi Gyimah, and E.O.B. It was relocated to Triple X early 2024 ahead of the commencement of road construction.
