TRIPLE X - HOTEL

Triple X - Hotel is one of the two hotels of Adjosec Investment Limited. It is located along Accra - Cape Goast Road, Ghana. The hotel is a 5-star hotel with a capacity of over 50 rooms. It has a restaurant, a bar, a swimming pool, a gym, and a spa. The hotel is ideal for business and leisure travelers. It is also suitable for conferences, meetings, and events.
Triple X - Hotel, bearing the name of the company, is a symbol of the company's commitment to providing quality services to its clients. It is a reflection of the company's vision to be a leading investment company in Ghana and beyond. The hotel is a testament to the company's dedication to excellence and its desire to provide its clients with the best possible experience.

Don't miss the opportunity to experience the luxury and comfort of Triple X - Hotel. Book your stay today and enjoy the best of what Ghana has to offer.
For more information, please contact us at +233 509 557 309 . You can also book your stay online through our website at Book . We look forward to welcoming you to Triple X - Hotel and providing you with an unforgettable experience.
