About The C.E.O

Mr. Kwabena Owusu on the left is the Chief Executive Officer (C.E.O) of ADJOSEC INVESTMENT LIMITED in Buduburam Liberia Camp. He is an Ashanti having background from Kumasi in Central Ghana , West Africa. He is a business man and an investor, mostly interested in improving lives of people around him. He grew up as a hustler in Markola, the slum of Agbobloshie Market of Ghana. Investing in the youths and helping in lifting people through is his top priority because he knows how though life can be since he has the ghetto experience.
