BIOGRAPHY

Mr. Kwabena Owusu on the left, was born February 2nd, 1968 in Kumasi , nothern Ghana. He is a business man and an investor who is focused driven on improving lives of people mostly in need. While growing up in the slum of Agbobloshie Market of Ghana, he hustled to make life. Investing in the youths and helping in lifting people through is his top priority since his life began at the ghetto .

Mrs. Dorcas Boakye is his wife and current Manageress of ADJOSEC INVESTMENT LIMITED in Buduburam, Gomoa-East District Central Ghana. He has a daughter with her.
