TRIPLE X - BAR & RESTAURANT

Triple X - Bar is the first and oldest branch of ADJOSEC INVESTMENT LIMITED (XXX). It is this bar the name Triple X derived. Everyone living at Buduburam in the 90s knew very much about this bar. It is the first every chop bar and entertainment center in the region, which accommodated over 1000 individuals in a single occasion. It is at this bar, Club Brewery Limited used as advertising partnership to introduce its just made beers (Club and Shandy) in the region.

Triple X - Bar and Restaurant as a long history with so many dwellers and inhabitants of Liberia Camp both those at home and in the diaspora. Due to its inappropriate management of staff, it's not as active as it was before. But it is still standing awaiting the completion of the road construction to resume operations.
