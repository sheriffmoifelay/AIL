ADJOSEC INVESTMENT LIMITED

Adjosec Investmwnt Limited is a company in Buduburam mostly known as Triple X also written as XXX.
Triple X since early 21st century has been the first and best entertainment center in Buduburam Liberia Camp, entertaining both Ghannains, the Refugees from Liberia, and many other foreign nationals from the neighboring countries including Sierra Leone, Togo, Benin and Nigeria.
It is one of the modern developed infrastructures in the region. It helps most youths around its surrounding providing employment opportunities for as low as 16 years. With its various departments, employees find suitable positions under so many circumstances such as age, address, gender and nationalities. At Triple X, employment is strictly based on personal credentials of individual; what a person bring on board to help grow the company.

Visit the various departments to explore its products and services. Recruitment is always ongoing as the company served to train and help more youths off the street and struggling travellers finding means to settle or continue; but only if the serious ones. For more informations of the available vacancies, visit xxxjobs@buduburam to view and follow the process to apply.

The Wholesale / the Depot of the Company, plays a crucial role in the everyday affairs receiving most of the goods in large quantities that the company sells. Its an annex where products of different companies are received, sorted and filtered before distributing to other departments of the company. Though it is a new branch, it has been one of the most important branch of the company.

The Supermarket / the retailling of the company, is the best in the region now; a mini-mall providing almost everything for shopping.
Commonly known as X-Mart, its the most visited shopping center in Buduburam since its creation. It was relocated from the Liberia Camp where its initial building was demolished by the road construction projects which marked the building victim.





Triple X Hotel which carries the company's most recognized name, is one of the two hotel departments of the company. With well furnished rooms, it is the most recommended hotel many travellers choose to stay for better relaxation and comfortable lodging. It is located by the main road - Accra to Cape Coast Road which makes its accessibility more conducive to customers. With an inside Bar and Restaurant delivering both local and continental dishes, people feel at home while lodged. And customer service is the top priority of every staff allowing frequent and more visits.
